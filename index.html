Welcome to my page!

Hello! My name is Dylan Huston, but if you got here from the landing page I will assume you knew that! I currently attend the University of Oklahoma as a Computer Engineering student. I have been attenting OU since Spring 2025, and am expected to graduate in December of 2026. Before OU I recieved an associates in Enterprise Development from Rose State College in 2024. I have also attended classes at Oklahoma State University, University of Central Oklahoma, Oklahoma City Community College, and Tulsa Community College. But ultimately my heart landed at OU.

While at the University of Oklahoma, I learned that I really like designing circuitry. The feeling that you get when something works it one of the best feelings in the world. Outside of designing electronics, I was also able to reach much deeper than I thought I would when I worked with researchers on writing a paper on photonic devices and their future applications. I always knew transistors were very small but I had no idea that photonic devices, like micro ring resonators, can be just a few micrometers wide. The paper that contains my discussion on the topic is currently in the process of being published!

Outside of the education realm, I like to work on small projects at home. The main thing that I am working on right now is getting my server running again! I got a good deal on fans and now it is far too loud, so it is powered down until I can find a solution for volume without removing all those extra fans. Once that gets back up look forward to a few updates on my projects page!

In the meantime take a look around the page for now, it is still a work in progress but I am updating as quickly as I am able! Feel free to contact me if you wish using the "Contact" button on the home page or you can click here.
